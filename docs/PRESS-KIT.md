# KritiKart — Press Kit (EPK)

## Factsheet

- **Title**: KritiKart
- **Genre**: 3D arcade kart racing
- **Platforms**: Android (arm64), Web (WASM)
- **Engine**: Godot 4.7.2-stable (Jolt Physics)
- **Version**: 0.1.0 (first release)
- **Developer**: MaximoSEO studio (Tomerake)
- **Package**: `com.maximoseo.kritikart` (signed release builds)
- **Repository**: github.com/maximoseo/kritikart (private)

## Gameplay

- 3-lap circuit races with **6 racers**: the player plus 5 AI opponents, each with a
  distinct driving personality (technician, bully, showoff).
- Three difficulty tiers (easy / medium / hard) with player assists and rubber-band
  AI pacing, so every race stays close.
- The launch track — **Storm Coast** — is a cliffside circuit with wet asphalt,
  storm lighting, and jump set-pieces.
- Countdown start, checkpoints, wrong-way detection, live position tracking, and a
  full race-results screen.
- Cloud leaderboards per track & difficulty (Supabase), offline-first.
